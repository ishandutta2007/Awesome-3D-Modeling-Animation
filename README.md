# Awesome-3D-Modeling-Animation

# Top 3D Modeling & Animation Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of Commercial Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on 3D Modeling, Character Animation, Sculpting, Rigging, Simulation & Content Creation Pipelines*
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial platforms** and **open-source projects** for **3D Modeling & Animation**. These tools cover polygonal and subdivision modeling, digital sculpting, rigging, keyframe and procedural animation, simulation, UV mapping, and full content-creation pipelines used in film, games, product design, and visualization.

**Examples** include Autodesk Maya, Autodesk 3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Blender Studio, SideFX Houdini, Modo, LightWave 3D, ZBrush, Substance 3D, and SketchUp Pro (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: **Blender** is the dominant open-source 3D creation suite and a viable production alternative for many workflows. Complementary tools such as **FreeCAD**, **MeshLab**, **MakeHuman**, **Wings 3D**, and related projects expand the open ecosystem. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Autodesk Maya](https://www.autodesk.com/products/maya/overview)**  
  Industry-standard 3D modeling, animation, and rendering software used extensively in film, television, and game production for characters, environments, and complex pipelines.

- **[Autodesk 3ds Max](https://www.autodesk.com/products/3ds-max/overview)**  
  Professional 3D modeling, animation, and rendering tool especially popular in architecture, games, and visual effects, with strong modeling and plugin ecosystems.

- **[Cinema 4D](https://www.maxon.net/en/cinema-4d)**  
  Artist-friendly 3D package from Maxon known for motion graphics, procedural modeling, and fast, intuitive animation and rendering workflows.

- **[Blender Studio / Blender Cloud services](https://studio.blender.org/)**  
  Commercial production, training, and cloud offerings built around the open-source Blender project (assets, open movies, collaborative features).

- **[SideFX Houdini](https://www.sidefx.com/products/houdini/)**  
  Node-based procedural 3D application renowned for VFX, simulation (fluids, destruction, particles), and highly technical, production-scale pipelines.

- **[Modo](https://www.foundry.com/products/modo)**  
  Direct modeling and animation tool from Foundry with strong mesh modeling, sculpting, and look-development capabilities.

- **[LightWave 3D](https://www.lightwave3d.com/)**  
  Long-standing 3D modeling and animation package historically used in film and broadcast, offering integrated modeling, layout, and rendering.

- **[ZBrush](https://www.maxon.net/en/zbrush)**  
  Industry-leading digital sculpting and painting application used for high-detail character and creature work in games and film.

- **[Substance 3D (Adobe)](https://www.adobe.com/products/substance3d.html)**  
  Suite of tools for material authoring, texturing, and look development (Painter, Designer, Sampler) widely integrated into modern 3D pipelines.

- **[SketchUp Pro](https://www.sketchup.com/)**  
  Accessible 3D modeling software popular in architecture, interior design, and concept work, with a large extension ecosystem and cloud collaboration features.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Blender](https://github.com/blender/blender)**  
  The official Blender project repository — a complete free and open-source 3D creation suite covering modeling, sculpting, animation, simulation, rendering, compositing, and video editing. The primary open alternative to commercial DCC tools.

- **[FreeCAD](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD)**  
  Open-source parametric 3D CAD modeler focused on mechanical design, architecture, and product design with a modular workbench system.

- **[MeshLab](https://github.com/cnr-isti-vclab/meshlab)**  
  Open-source system for processing and editing large unstructured 3D triangular meshes — useful for cleanup, remeshing, and scan data preparation.

- **[MakeHuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman)**  
  Open-source tool for creating realistic 3D human characters with parametric control over body shape, proportions, and basic clothing.

- **[Wings 3D](https://github.com/dgud/wings)**  
  Open-source subdivision modeler focused on clean polygonal modeling with a straightforward interface.

- **[OpenSCAD](https://github.com/openscad/openscad)**  
  Open-source script-based 3D CAD modeler ideal for parametric and programmatic solid modeling.

- **[Instant Meshes](https://github.com/wjakob/instant-meshes)**  
  Open-source research tool for fast, high-quality quad-dominant remeshing of triangular meshes.

- **[Dust3D](https://github.com/huxingyi/dust3d)**  
  Open-source 3D modeling software aimed at rapid game-asset creation with auto-rigging and UV features.

- **[Houdini-related open tools & HDAs communities](https://www.sidefx.com/)**  
  Community-driven open assets and learning resources that complement procedural workflows (while Houdini itself remains commercial).

- **[Documentation and Blender production guides](https://docs.blender.org/)**  
  Official manuals, open-movie pipeline documentation, and community tutorials for professional use of Blender.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **Blender** as the all-in-one open replacement for modeling, animation, sculpting, and rendering.
- Choosing **FreeCAD** or **OpenSCAD** for precision CAD and parametric design.
- Employing **MeshLab** and **Instant Meshes** for scan processing and retopology support.
- Generating base characters with **MakeHuman** and refining them in Blender.
- Combining open modeling tools with free texture/material resources and open render engines (Cycles, EEVEE).
- Accepting that commercial packages (Maya, 3ds Max, Houdini, ZBrush, Substance 3D, Cinema 4D) still dominate high-end film/VFX pipelines, specialized sculpting, and enterprise support contracts.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Create and animate primarily in **Blender** → use **FreeCAD**/OpenSCAD for hard-surface/CAD work → clean meshes with **MeshLab** → texture with open or Substance-compatible workflows → render with Cycles/EEVEE or external engines. Suitable for independents, studios adopting open pipelines, education, and research. Large VFX and game studios often still rely on Maya, Houdini, and ZBrush for specialized departments and pipeline integration.
